Problems We Solve Every Day

You have probably run into at least two of these. Here is what each one looks like from the inside, and what we actually do about it.

"We post when somebody remembers to post."

Most companies make video when a launch forces them to, and then go quiet for six weeks. We build a calendar and get it approved before the month starts, so something goes out every week whether or not anyone chases it.

"Our founder is the best thing we have and the hardest person to book."

So we batch. One shoot day gives us four to six weeks of content, which means your founder spends a few hours on camera once, instead of a few hours every single week.

"The footage is already sitting in a folder somewhere."

It usually is. Webinar recordings, conference talks, customer calls, old product demos. Most companies have far more usable footage than they think, and we go through all of it and cut the parts worth cutting.

"Nobody can tell us whether any of it worked."

Every month you get a report that says what went out, what got watched, what got ignored, and what we are changing next month because of it. No dashboard login required, because we read it to you on the call.

What We Do Not Do

It is worth saying the limits out loud, so you can rule us out early if we are the wrong studio for you.

01 · One-offs

We do not take one-off videos.

A single video does not build anything. It picks up some views, the account goes quiet again, and you are back where you started. The whole point of a system is that the next video is already scripted while this one is going out.

02 · Capacity

We do not take ten new clients at once.

We take two or three a quarter. The editors, writers and designers listed above are real people with real capacity, and the moment we oversell them you get slower turnarounds and worse cuts.

03 · Decks

We do not hand over a strategy deck and leave.

Plenty of agencies sell the content strategy as the deliverable. Strategy is the cheap part. The expensive, unglamorous part is producing and posting every week for a year, and that is the part we actually do.

If any of those three is a problem, we are the wrong studio, and we will tell you on the first call.
Better to find out in 20 minutes than in month two.
